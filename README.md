# Business Insights 360 – Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
Business Insights 360 is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze business performance across different areas. The dashboard provides multiple views, including **Sales, Finance, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive** analysis.

It helps users understand important business metrics such as **Net Sales, Gross Margin, P&L, Forecast Accuracy, and Risk Profile** through interactive visualizations and filters.

The project focuses on transforming business data into clear and meaningful insights that can support better business analysis and decision-making.


## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform business data into meaningful insights using interactive dashboards and KPI analysis.

The dashboard helps users analyze:

* Sales performance
* Financial performance
* Customer and product performance
* Marketing performance
* Supply chain and forecast accuracy
* Overall executive-level business insights

##  Dashboard Views

### Executive View

Provides a top-level overview of important business insights by consolidating information from different business dimensions.

### Finance View

Provides analysis of financial performance, including **P&L-related analysis** for customers, products, countries, and different time periods.

### Sales View

Helps analyze customer and product performance using metrics such as:

* Net Sales
* Gross Margin
* Profitability
* Growth

It also provides a profitability and growth matrix for deeper analysis.

### Marketing View

Provides analysis related to:

* Forecast Accuracy
* Net Error
* Risk Profile

The analysis can be performed across products, segments, categories, customers, and other business dimensions.

### Supply Chain View

Provides insights into supply chain performance and forecast-related metrics to support business analysis.
